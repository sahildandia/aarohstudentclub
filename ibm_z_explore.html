About IBM Z Explore Student Community

An IEEE student chapter is a university-based club that connects students in engineering, computer science, and other related fields with the broader IEEE community, offering opportunities for professional development, networking, and hands-on experience. These chapters organize events, host conferences, collaborate on projects, and provide a platform for students

The club aims to engage students in practical experiences and knowledge-building activities that enhance their engineering skills, encourage creativity, and develop confidence and teamwork—preparing them for success in both academic and professional arenas.

Under the mentorship of Ms.B.Sharmiladevi, AP/EEE, our club members get opportunities to work on real-world problems, interact with industry professionals, and participate in national and international competitions.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To inspire and empower students and professionals in the field of electrical, electronics, and computing technologies to innovate, lead, and contribute to the global community.

Mission

To provide members with opportunities to expand their technical knowledge through workshops, seminars, and conferences.

To create a platform where members can connect with professionals, industry experts, and peers globally.
