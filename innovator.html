About Innovators Tech Forum Club

The Innovators Tech Forum Club (Powered by Microsoft Student Ambassador) is a global initiative that empowers students to become leaders and community builders by sharing their passion for Microsoft technologies and fostering technical and soft skills development.

Ambassadors gain exclusive access to Microsoft resources, mentor from industry experts, and organize events to help peers learn about and use technologies like cloud computing and AI. The program offers a path for students to grow professionally, network, and build a strong portfolio for future career opportunities in the tech industry.

Under the mentorship of Mrs.P.Priya, AP/CSE, our club members get opportunities to work on real-world problems, interact with industry professionals, and participate in national and international competitions.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To build a platform where students sharpen their skills and gain confidence to excel in technical challenges and real-world applications.

Mission

1. To empower students with strong coding and problem-solving abilities through continuous learning.

2. To nurture innovation and industry-ready skills by organizing technical and career-focused events.
